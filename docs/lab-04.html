True Color images are best for seeing the landscape in a familiar way. It helps us identify objects because we are used to seeing them in RGB. False color 432 images highlight vegetation because NIR is highly reflective in plants. False color 654 highlights water features by making them very dark relative to everything else. False color 764 highlights urban infrastructure.

Unlike the other 4 indices, NDVI is designed to highlight vegetation, not water. However, water has a very low NDVI so it’s still outlines the river quite well. Out of the 4 water indices, MNDWI does the best job of clearly delineating the river.
